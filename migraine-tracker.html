01 / CONTEXT

The situation

Many tracking tools ask for detailed input at exactly the wrong moment.

During a migraine, pain, fatigue or brain fog can make even simple documentation feel like effort. That effort can become the reason nothing gets recorded at all.

The project began with a practical need: keeping a reliable record of migraine frequency for future appointments. Once an attack had passed, I sometimes remembered it too late to reconstruct the exact date.

What is the smallest realistic action that still creates value?
02 / CORE INTERACTION

One tap is enough

Recording that a migraine happened is separate from describing it.

The main action is available immediately when the app opens. A single tap saves the event. Severity, symptoms, medication and other details are never required for a valid entry.

03 / HISTORY

A simple record

Each entry appears in a calendar. Recorded days are marked directly in the month view, alongside a quiet count of migraine days. Past dates can also be logged retroactively.

04 / TRADE-OFF

Making room for detail

Adding more information creates more choices — exactly what the core interaction tries to remove.

Detail Mode therefore stays optional. People who want richer records can add severity, symptoms, medication, duration and notes in a step-based flow; the initial log is already complete. Personalization reduces later decisions by showing only relevant symptoms and medications.

An unresolved question remains: how can people discover Detail Mode without adding friction or visual clutter to the first-use experience?

05 / VISUAL DESIGN

Less visual load

The restrained off-white, grayscale and muted sage palette was chosen with low-capacity moments in mind. Stronger visual emphasis is reserved for the most important action. Settings use familiar iOS conventions, while the primary screens have their own quieter visual language.

The interface was developed iteratively in SwiftUI rather than through a conventional wireframe-first process. Design decisions were tested in the running app, adjusted and often simplified.

06 / NEXT STEPS

Where it stands

The core one-tap interaction is in place. Detail Mode discoverability, sparse entries and the default customization experience still need testing. A doctor-friendly PDF export is planned; its exact format is not finalized.

The central lesson so far: the project improved whenever I reduced requirements, simplified inputs and protected the core interaction from unnecessary complexity.
